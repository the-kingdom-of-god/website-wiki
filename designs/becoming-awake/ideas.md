# KOG Website Design Direction

## Three starting directions

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sanctuary Editorial | A calm, image-led faith community site inspired by independent print journals and quiet art direction. It treats whitespace, metaphor, and invitation as active parts of the experience. | 0.07 |
| Open Table | A warm, approachable community hub shaped by handmade hospitality, soft material textures, and candid human connection. It makes participation feel simple and personal. | 0.04 |
| Living Monument | A sculptural digital identity built from monumental type, mineral colours, and spatial compositions that evoke permanence without feeling institutional. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach: Sanctuary Editorial

### Design Movement

**Contemporary faith editorialism**: the restraint of independent culture journals translated into a reverent, human digital gathering place. The design takes its structural cue from a high-end editorial catalogue but is wholly original in content, assets, and KOG branding.

### Core Principles

The experience will be **spacious rather than sparse**, using room and rhythm to let faith-centered ideas land. It will be **poetic but legible**, pairing evocative headlines with plain-language next steps. It will be **communal rather than institutional**, featuring people, participation, and simple invitations over corporate claims. Finally, it will feel **intentional in every detail**, using few materials and carefully controlled visual contrast.

### Color Philosophy

Warm ivory acts as the ground: open, daylight-like, and quietly ceremonial. A deep ink-black creates focus and continuity without harshness. Mineral blue brings a grounded sense of depth and trust, while a restrained oxidized-gold accent marks moments of welcome, movement, and response. These colors suggest a Kingdom already present in everyday life—not a distant or glossy ideal.

### Layout Paradigm

The page uses an **editorial field** rather than a centered marketing stack. Major sections slide between full-bleed photographic moments, left-anchored essay blocks, off-grid index cards, and an asymmetric invitation panel. An anchored vertical rule and occasional small-cap labels form a navigational thread down the page.

### Signature Elements

The design is held together by three recurring motifs: a small **rising-sun arc** symbol for KOG, fine-rule **editorial index lines** that label each chapter of the page, and **cropped image windows** that feel like selected pages from a visual journal. Subtle paper grain softens large color fields.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction should feel like a thoughtful invitation, not a sales funnel. Links gain an underline that grows from the left; image cards reveal an observational prompt on hover; the menu opens as a quiet index. Buttons use plain verbs such as “Find your place” and “Receive the journal.”

### Animation

Motion is slow, editorial, and limited to opacity and transform. On entry, text sections rise no more than 14px over 500ms using a soft ease-out; image windows subtly unmask from the bottom. The sun-arc mark rotates by a few degrees on hover. Motion is disabled for visitors who prefer reduced motion, and no interaction is delayed by animation.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** provides the contemplative display voice, set with large but compact line breaks. **Manrope** provides clear modern navigation, labels, and body copy. Display headlines use sentence case and a measured italic accent; all navigation, metadata, and category labels use compact uppercase tracking. This contrast lets KOG sound both considered and present.

### Brand Essence

**KOG is a faith-centered community for people seeking to practice the Kingdom of God in public, ordinary life.** Its personality is **grounded, generous, and awake**.

### Brand Voice

The voice is direct, observant, and hospitable. Headlines should name a possibility rather than make a promise; CTAs should invite a specific next step. Avoid insider language, performance claims, and vague uplift. Example lines: “Make room for a different kind of life.” and “Come as you are. Stay for what grows between us.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is a tightly spaced **KOG** with a small, horizon-like half-sun tucked into the open space of the O. The standalone mark is a bold half-sun above a single horizon line: clear at favicon scale, meaningful without relying on text, and not derivative of the reference site.

### Signature Brand Color

**Kingdom Mineral — #43646B**. A muted blue-green that is composed, memorable, and strong enough to identify KOG in a single field of color.

## Style Decisions

KOG imagery should prioritize ordinary embodied community: tables, streets, hands, shared work, meals, thresholds, and neighborhood life. Kingdom Mineral is the primary brand field and recognition color, while oxidized gold is reserved for italic emphasis, the sun mark, and key invitation moments. No visible interface copy should read as an internal note, placeholder, or product instruction; even form helper text must remain hospitable and editorial.
