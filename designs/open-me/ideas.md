# KOG — Kingdom of God

## Ground-truth reference

This project is a faithful recreation of the supplied Flying Papers age-gate screenshot, adapted for the KOG / Kingdom of God brand. The screenshot governs the composition: a single uninterrupted blue-violet field; a tiny centered wordmark at the top; an enormous warm-yellow stacked headline around the middle-lower region; a simple off-white, black-outlined peeking mascot at the headline seam; and a compact centered CTA stack beneath. The backup reference site confirms the same age-gate structure and interaction copy.

## Design philosophy

### Design Movement
Retro editorial poster design with 1970s comic-book display typography and intentionally sparse web composition.

### Core Principles
1. **Poster-first hierarchy:** treat the viewport as a single bold print composition rather than a conventional multi-section landing page.
2. **Graphic restraint:** use a flat field, one signature accent, and near-black ink so the hero remains instantly legible.
3. **Playful interruption:** the mascot breaks the headline seam and gives the page a wink without competing with the message.
4. **Responsive drama:** preserve generous negative space and strong scale contrast from mobile through desktop.

### Color Philosophy
A dusky blue-violet field creates a calm, contemplative atmosphere associated with night sky and reflection. Warm wheat-yellow typography injects optimism and human warmth. Black ink and soft ivory controls create the crisp, screen-printed contrast of the reference.

### Layout Paradigm
A tall, vertically staged poster: the wordmark anchors the top margin, the hero composition sits in a deliberate middle-lower band, and the rest of the page remains open and quiet. No card grid, no conventional nav, no excess content.

### Signature Elements
- Warm wheat-yellow, compressed all-caps display headline.
- Off-white peeking KOG mascot with black comic outline.
- Cream CTA with offset ink shadow and a small underlined secondary action.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel tactile and slightly mischievous: controls lift or nudge rather than glow; hover states sharpen contrast; the mascot makes a small bobbing peek; focus states are visible and clean.

### Animation
Use a gentle reveal on page load, a slow floating motion for the mascot, a 180–240ms button lift/press response, and an understated underline sweep on the secondary action. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System
Use a high-impact retro display face for the wordmark and headline, paired with a compact grotesk for controls and utility copy. Headline type is tightly tracked and oversized; controls are uppercase, letter-spaced, and intentionally small.

### Brand Essence
**KOG is a playful doorway into the Kingdom of God for curious people who want faith to feel alive, human, and welcoming.** Personality: curious, warm, unexpected.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, conversational, and lightly cheeky. CTAs are concise and invitational rather than formal.

Example lines:
- “HOW OPEN ARE YOU?”
- “COME SEE WHAT’S ON THE OTHER SIDE”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact KOG monogram built as a bold circular mark with a small crown/halo notch, paired with a custom text wordmark rather than a default UI label.

### Signature Brand Color
**Kingdom Wheat** — `#F0C873`.
