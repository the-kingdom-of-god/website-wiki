# KOG design direction

## Ground-truth reference

The supplied screenshot is the primary visual reference: a full-screen pale pink loading state with a centered black condensed `85%` label. The reference website establishes the secondary interaction language: oversized condensed headlines, full-bleed photography, floating navigation, bold pink/black contrast, and a long-scroll editorial narrative with clear section anchors.

## Chosen approach: Hot-pink editorial collage

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial brutalism with fashion-magazine pacing and street-poster energy.

### Core Principles
1. **Overscale creates conviction:** headlines behave like visual objects, not ordinary copy.
2. **Pink is the field:** the brand owns a warm bubblegum pink as an expansive canvas, while black provides hard contrast.
3. **Image-led storytelling:** every major section gets one strong photographic or graphic moment instead of a gallery of small cards.
4. **Navigation stays light:** a minimal floating rail helps users move through the story without competing with the content.

### Color Philosophy
Bubblegum pink communicates joy, openness, and a non-corporate spiritual community. Near-black gives the page weight and makes the typography feel printed rather than decorative. White appears only as a relief color inside dense black moments.

### Layout Paradigm
A vertical editorial scroll with alternating full-bleed imagery, offset text blocks, large typographic interludes, and a persistent edge navigation. Avoid a centered marketing grid; let sections drift between left, right, and edge-aligned compositions.

### Signature Elements
A thin black progress rail, condensed all-caps headlines with broken line rhythm, and a small cross/starburst mark used as a visual punctuation glyph.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning pages in a tactile zine: links underline or invert quickly, menu items reveal their target section, and the primary CTA uses a sharp black block rather than a soft pill.

### Animation
The initial loader holds on the pink field for a brief, calm beat, counting from `0%` to `85%` before the page reveals. Section headlines lift in from below with a short stagger; image crops shift gently on hover; navigation transitions use opacity and translate only. Respect reduced motion.

### Typography System
Use **Bebas Neue** for display and navigation, with **DM Sans** for body copy and supporting labels. Headlines are tight, uppercase, and frequently split across lines. Body copy stays compact, conversational, and left-aligned.

### Brand Essence
KOG is a visual home for people practicing the Kingdom of God in ordinary life — a communal, art-forward alternative to polished institutional religion. Personality: **open, kinetic, grounded**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and slightly provocative; CTAs are active and human; microcopy is warm without being vague.

Example lines: **“MAKE ROOM FOR THE KINGDOM.”** / **“COME AS YOU ARE. LEAVE WITH A NEXT STEP.”**

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact black cross/starburst symbol paired with a custom stacked `KOG` wordmark in an extended condensed treatment. The symbol should work independently as a favicon and section marker.

### Signature Brand Color
`#F2B4D1` — a warm bubblegum pink that is softer than hot magenta but strong enough to hold a full viewport.

## Style Decisions
- Keep the loader visually faithful to the supplied image: pink field, centered black percentage, no extra decoration.
- Treat the resolved page as the purposeful continuation of that loader, not as a separate visual system.
- Use full-bleed moments and oversized type rather than repeated card grids.
