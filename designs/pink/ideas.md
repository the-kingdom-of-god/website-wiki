# KOG design direction

## Ground-truth reference
This project is a screenshot-faithful recreation of the supplied KOG reference state. The primary composition is a tall, full-bleed hot-pink preloader screen: a minimal progress readout in the upper corners, a small white crown mark near the visual center, and a compact white copyright disclaimer anchored at the bottom. The supplied screenshot overrides any exploratory style direction; the reference page is used only to clarify that this is a preloader-inspired hero.

## Design Movement
Digital editorial minimalism with a devotional, ceremonial tone. The experience should feel like a single vivid field of color with tiny, deliberate signals of progress and identity.

## Core Principles
1. Preserve the extreme negative space and single-color field of the reference.
2. Keep the hierarchy microscopic and precise: progress, crown, disclaimer.
3. Use texture and tonal variation only when it remains nearly subliminal.
4. Motion should feel patient and calm, never flashy or game-like.

## Color Philosophy
Pink is the entire emotional environment: warm, energetic, youthful, and unmistakable. A two-stop rose/magenta gradient prevents the field from feeling flat while retaining the screenshot's visual simplicity. White is reserved for utility and identity so every mark reads like a quiet overlay.

## Layout Paradigm
A fixed viewport composition rather than a conventional content grid. Progress labels sit on the top baseline, the crown is centered in the open field, and the disclaimer occupies a narrow bottom measure with intentional line breaks.

## Signature Elements
A tiny outlined crown rendered as a custom SVG; a soft, nearly invisible vertical color drift; and a subtle grain veil that keeps the color field tactile.

## Interaction Philosophy
The first frame is intentionally quiet. Loading progress advances gently, the crown gives a slight breathing motion, and the page can transition to a restrained KOG title state without disturbing the initial visual match.

## Animation
Use opacity and transform only. Progress increments ease smoothly toward 100; the crown floats by a few pixels on a long cycle; the disclaimer stays still. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep the initial screenshot visually stable.

## Typography System
Use a compact geometric sans-serif stack with `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks. Utility numerals are small and light; the disclaimer is set in a readable 11–12 px measure with tight leading and centered alignment.

## Brand Essence
KOG is a quiet digital threshold for people seeking a vivid, modern expression of the Kingdom of God; it is minimal, luminous, and intimate.

## Brand Voice
Headlines and microcopy are spare, direct, and contemplative. Example lines: `A kingdom is already here.` and `Stay with the light.`

## Wordmark & Logo
The mark is an abstract open crown: three angular peaks, a shallow basin, and a small inner notch, drawn as a white monoline symbol rather than typeset text.

## Signature Brand Color
`#F55EAB` — a vivid raspberry-pink field that owns the first impression.
