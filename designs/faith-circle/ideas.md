# KOG Design Direction

## Chosen approach: Sacred Modernism

**Design Movement:** Contemporary editorial modernism blended with quiet liturgical minimalism.

**Core Principles:**
- Treat photography and typography as the primary structure, not decoration.
- Use warm natural materials and one vivid cobalt accent to make the brand feel grounded yet alive.
- Build tension through oversized type, off-center composition, and long scroll pacing.
- Keep interactions tactile, quick, and legible: small movement, clear intent, no gimmicks.

**Color Philosophy:** Warm bone and parchment backgrounds create a sense of welcome and permanence; charcoal gives the page weight; deep olive connects the brand to earth and growth; cobalt blue signals action and contemporary energy. The palette should feel human, not corporate.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed image chapters alternate with offset editorial blocks. Content often aligns to a left rail while headlines drift wider than the body copy. Avoid symmetric, centered stacks whenever a side-by-side or anchored composition communicates better.

**Signature Elements:** A cobalt route-line motif, pill-shaped utility links with arrow cues, and thin rule lines used as visual breath between sections.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Hover states should feel like a gentle invitation forward: labels shift a few pixels, arrows travel, image crops breathe, and cards lift only slightly. Every interactive item must have a visible focus treatment.

**Animation:** Use 180–280ms ease-out transitions for controls, image scale 1.03 on hover, staggered reveal for card groups, and a slow hero drift. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and never rely on motion to convey essential information.

**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond for expressive display headlines; DM Sans for navigation, labels, body copy, and metadata. Headlines use tight tracking and generous line-height; body copy stays compact and airy.

**Brand Essence:** KOG is a modern movement helping people live the Kingdom of God through presence, practice, and shared action — for communities seeking faith with a public shape. Personality: grounded, luminous, invitational.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are declarative and poetic; CTAs are direct but warm; microcopy is calm and specific.
- “A different kind of kingdom is already here.”
- “Find your next faithful step.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** Use a simple KOG wordmark paired with a three-arch mark suggesting people, shelter, and a rising path. Keep the mark graphic and compact so it reads clearly in the utility nav.

**Signature Brand Color:** Cobalt route blue `#2457F5`.
