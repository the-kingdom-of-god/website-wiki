# KOG Design Direction

## Ground-truth reference
The reference site establishes the visual benchmark: a black editorial canvas, fixed compact navigation, oversized white typography, sparse copy, surprising sculptural imagery, thin utility labels, and scroll-led pacing. KOG should preserve that composition language while translating the content into a spiritual / cultural movement.

## Chosen approach: Sacred Modernism

### Design Movement
Sacred modernism: a collision of Swiss editorial restraint, art-direction-led creative studio websites, and contemporary spiritual symbolism.

### Core Principles
1. **Ritual through pacing** — generous black space, slow reveals, and repeated marks make scrolling feel intentional.
2. **Clarity with friction** — typography is simple, but scale, cropping, and asymmetry create tension.
3. **Symbol before explanation** — imagery should suggest meaning before copy defines it.
4. **Warmth inside severity** — bone-white type and a single acid chartreuse accent keep the dark system human and alive.

### Color Philosophy
Ink black is the field of possibility and contemplation. Bone white feels archival rather than sterile. Chartreuse is the signature spark: a signal of life, renewal, and forward motion. Muted clay and smoke-gray tones appear only inside artwork, never as competing UI accents.

### Layout Paradigm
A vertical, scroll-ritual composition with oversized full-bleed statements, offset content columns, vertical labels, and irregular artwork placements. Avoid centered marketing blocks; let content enter from edges and sit against purposeful voids.

### Signature Elements
- Acid-chartreuse micro-indexes and cursor-like markers.
- Cropped, tactile 3D relics: flame, stone, halo, and folded fabric.
- Oversized words broken across lines with occasional vertical writing.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like opening a door or shifting perspective. Links brighten and slide a few pixels; the menu opens as a full-screen black veil with large text; work tiles reveal a second phrase on hover. Buttons use tactile press feedback, never bouncy ornament.

### Animation
Use 220–700ms cubic-bezier reveals, slight vertical drift, and opacity transitions. The loader begins as a nearly empty black frame, then resolves the KOG mark and a numeric progress readout. Ambient artwork drifts subtly; primary copy reveals in staggered lines. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

### Typography System
Display: **Bebas Neue** for compressed, poster-like headlines. Body/UI: **Space Grotesk** for readable editorial copy and labels. Headlines use large tracking-tight uppercase with occasional italic emphasis; metadata stays small, uppercase, and widely tracked.

### Brand Essence
KOG is a living creative movement for people seeking faith, culture, and action in the same frame — distinct because it treats belief as a visual language, not a passive category.

Personality: **restless, generous, lucid**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative, poetic, and slightly provocative. CTAs are direct and physical. Microcopy is short, human, and never corporate.

Example lines:
- “Make room for what moves.”
- “A faith with its sleeves rolled up.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact monogram built from two mirrored K-shaped cuts forming a doorway / crown silhouette. The mark should work as a standalone favicon and as a large loading glyph.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Chartreuse — #C8FF2E**.
