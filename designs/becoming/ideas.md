# KOG design direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Cinematic Editorial / Sacred Modernism
Very large type, disciplined white space, and a slow-to-fast reveal rhythm turn the page into an experience rather than a brochure.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Field Notes / Living Archive
A tactile, notebook-like system uses annotations, image studies, and documentary fragments to make KOG feel observed and human.
Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Night Signal / Future Liturgy
A darker, more atmospheric direction uses blue-black surfaces and warm signal colors to frame KOG as a cultural beacon.
Probability: 0.02

## Chosen approach

### Design Movement
Cinematic Editorial / Sacred Modernism: the disciplined restraint of a contemporary architecture journal combined with the emotional pacing of a film title sequence.

### Core Principles
1. Scale contrast: tiny signals and intimate copy are allowed to sit inside enormous fields.
2. Purposeful asymmetry: the page should feel composed, not tiled.
3. Material honesty: surfaces, grain, shadows, and photography carry meaning.
4. Motion as theology: scroll reveals are calm, legible, and cumulative rather than decorative.

### Color Philosophy
The canvas begins chalk white to create room for attention. Ink blue carries conviction and depth; vermilion is a human signal used sparingly for action, warmth, and interruption. A mineral gray keeps the system grounded.

### Layout Paradigm
A vertical filmstrip rather than a card grid: large narrative chapters, image studies that cross the content rail, a centered scroll marker, and occasional edge-to-edge transitions.

### Signature Elements
A thin vertical progress rail, vermilion sun-disc mark, and redacted-looking metadata labels that make the site feel like a living creative archive.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should feel like a physical gesture: press, drag, reveal, enter. Hover states expose hidden metadata; the menu opens as a full-screen editorial overlay; scroll changes image scale and position rather than adding noisy effects.

### Animation
Use slow easing for scene changes and short, tactile transitions for controls. Hero imagery moves at a slower rate than copy. Respect reduced motion by removing scale and parallax while keeping content order and contrast.

### Typography System
Display: Space Grotesk, 700–800, aggressively tracked in large headlines. Body: DM Sans, 400–500, for calm readability. Labels: Space Grotesk, uppercase, 0.16em tracking.

### Brand Essence
KOG makes belief tangible through design, community, and cultural work for people who want a more generous future. Personality: visionary, grounded, magnetic.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and slightly poetic. CTAs are direct, never salesy. Microcopy is observant and specific.
Example lines: “Make the invisible feel close.” / “Enter the work.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A custom KOG wordmark in a compressed grotesk paired with a geometric sun-door emblem: a red vertical opening with a blue counterform.

### Signature Brand Color
Vermilion signal: #F0442E.

## Style Decisions

The KOG image direction stays focused on cinematic studies of architecture, thresholds, light, gatherings, and material details. The sun-door emblem and thin vertical progress rail are recurring structural devices, appearing in section metadata and the closing chapter rather than only in the header. Vermilion #F0442E remains a sacred signal color for action, interruption, metadata, and key words; the closing field is now ink blue with a vermilion sun-disc so the ending feels ceremonial rather than promotional.
